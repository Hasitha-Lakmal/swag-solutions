Features

Complete accounts, made simple for shop staff.

Daily cash and bank

Income, expenses and transfers for every outlet, cash count and day close, so the cash in the drawer always matches the books.

Cheques under control

Issued and received cheques, realise and bounce, undated cheques, search and filters, and bank reconciliation.

Suppliers and customers

Bill-wise payments and receipts, payment vouchers, advances, returns with debit and credit notes, statements and aging.

Invoices and quotations

Professional invoices, quotations and orders with your logo, outlet address and bank details.

True profit and reports

Profit and loss with stock value, balance sheet, cash flow and daily summary. See the real gross profit, not just sales.

HR and payroll

Monthly payroll with EPF and ETF, no-pay, OT and allowances, payslips on A4 or thermal, staff loans, savings and leave.

Loans and fixed assets

Loan and leasing installments split into capital and interest, fixed assets with depreciation, and year-end closing.

Several PCs and outlets

One PC is the server and starts with Windows; the other PCs connect to it, in the same shop or at a far outlet over the internet.

Users and audit log

Admin, manager, cashier or custom users with their own add, edit and delete rights. Every change is recorded.

Automatic backups

Backups every few hours to any folder, including Google Drive or OneDrive, with one-click restore.

Windows may show “Windows protected your PC” because Zenta is new. Click More info and then Run anyway. Zenta installs for the current user and does not need admin rights.
